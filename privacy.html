Analytics & Privacy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This is a personal academic website. It does not provide user accounts, advertising, e-commerce, or forms that ask visitors to submit personal information. Analytics are used only to understand aggregate site usage and improve the presentation of research and academic content.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for privacy-focused traffic and performance measurement. Cloudflare states that its Web Analytics product does not use cookies to collect usage metrics and does not collect or use visitors' personal data. This measurement remains enabled independently of the optional analytics preference below.

Optional analytics: Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) uses Google's consent mode. The Google tag loads in the page head so that consent state can be handled reliably, but analytics storage and all advertising-related storage are denied unless a visitor chooses Accept analytics. This site disables GA4's automatic page view and does not intentionally send detailed GA4 page-view or custom interaction events until analytics consent has been granted. Microsoft Clarity is loaded only after a visitor chooses Accept analytics.

  • Google Analytics 4 is used to measure page visits and selected interactions, such as visits to major sections and clicks on the CV, GitHub profile, project code, and contact links.
  • Microsoft Clarity is used to understand anonymous interaction patterns through tools such as heatmaps and session recordings.

This website does not intentionally send names, email addresses, or other directly identifying information to these analytics services. Email-link events record only that an academic or personal email link was clicked and where the link appeared; the email address and mailto URL are not sent as custom GA4 event parameters. Email links and the contact-link area are also explicitly masked for Microsoft Clarity. Advertising storage and personalization signals are not enabled by this implementation.

Your choice

You can accept or reject optional analytics. Your choice is stored locally in your browser under analytics_consent so the site can remember it on later visits. If you reject optional analytics, GA4 remains in consent-denied mode without Analytics cookies or detailed site-interaction events, while Microsoft Clarity is not loaded. You can change your choice at any time using Analytics preferences in the footer.

Third-party information

For more information about how these services handle data, see the official privacy and product documentation from Cloudflare, Google, and Microsoft.
