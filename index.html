Jun 2026 – Present CUHK · Computer Science & Engineering

Video Diffusion Models toward World-Model Capabilities

Supervisor: Prof. Shengchao Liu

I investigate whether pretrained video diffusion models encode usable physical dynamics, with the broader goal of extending visual generative models toward world-model capabilities for prediction and physical reasoning.

  • Built controlled motion and two-object interaction benchmarks and a reproducible CogVideoX-2B representation-analysis pipeline.
  • Mapped direction, speed, optical flow, and contact information across Transformer layers, diffusion noise levels, and readout strategies using linear probes, ID/OOD evaluation, and negative controls.
  • Best controlled ID results reached approximately 1.09° direction error and R² = 0.988 for speed.
  • Designed representation-level interventions to test whether decodable physical information is merely present or functionally used by the model.
Jan 2026 – Jul 2026 Multimodal Learning Research Project

Sample-Wise Dynamic Prompt Truncation for Multimodal Learning

Supervisor: Prof. David P. Woodruff

I studied whether prompt length can be treated as an adaptive resource in parameter-efficient multimodal learning instead of assigning every sample the same fixed prompt budget.

  • Extended a ViT-based Mixture of Prompt Experts (MoPE) classifier with a layer-wise gating mechanism that dynamically determines retained visual prompt length.
  • Adapted the framework to a derived IU X-Ray chest X-ray classification task and implemented controlled fixed-versus-adaptive prompting experiments.
  • In the recorded experiment, reduced the effective visual prompt budget from six tokens to one while maintaining validation performance.
  • Best validation accuracy increased from 0.8969 to 0.8996; final validation accuracy increased from 0.8802 to 0.8963.
